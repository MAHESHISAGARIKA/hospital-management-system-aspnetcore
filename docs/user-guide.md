# User guide

Sign in with the account issued by your administrator. Open **Dashboard** for your role's summaries and use the navigation to open permitted modules. Sign out when finished, especially on shared workstations.

| Role | Typical workflow |
| --- | --- |
| Administrator | Manage accounts and roles, maintain employee profiles and departments, review staff leave, and open operational reports. |
| Receptionist | Register patients, manage appointment scheduling and check-in, and use outpatient and inpatient admission worklists as permitted. |
| Doctor | Open appointments linked to your profile, document permitted encounters, request laboratory tests, and manage patients in your assigned clinical scope. |
| Nurse | Open assigned inpatient care and record permitted observations. |
| Laboratory Staff | Open the laboratory queue, process samples, enter results and follow the verification workflow. |
| Pharmacist | Manage inventory and batches, dispense finalized prescriptions, and review stock movements. |
| Accountant | Configure billable services, issue invoices, record payments and reversals, and review financial reports. |

Employees with an enabled linked profile can open **My staff profile** for attendance and leave. Reports use Colombo dates. Apply filters before printing or downloading a summary CSV. If an operation is denied, contact the administrator to check your account role or care assignment.

For isolated demo accounts, fictional data preparation and a timed walkthrough, see the [supervisor demonstration](supervisor-demo.md).
